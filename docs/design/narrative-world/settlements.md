# Dustland settlements & population placement

The main campaign's world map is populated intentionally, not randomly. People
cluster where people would cluster — water, roads, bunker light — and the
clusters are spaced so travel time paces the story's west-to-east difficulty
ramp.

## The three settlements

| Settlement | Where | Anchor | Role in pacing |
|------------|-------|--------|----------------|
| **Dawn Hall** | west (~10–17, 34–39) | the Hall, Nila's pump, trainers | Tutorial ring. Quests within a short walk (Ivo, Rella, Signal Tech to the north). |
| **Sparrow's Rest** | crossroads (~49–55, 22–28) | Cass's wagon + The Dry Bell cantina | Mid-journey rest right *before* the Duchess's toll (59,27) — the player refuels, hears rumors, then pays or fights. |
| **Last Ridge** | east (~79–85, 41–47) | Overlook bunker + Last Ridge Bedrolls | Final staging post. Respec vendor lives here. Everything past it is deep waste, the Duke, and the salt-flat finale. |

Between settlements, the Ridge bunker (60,50) is a lit waypoint, and hermits
(Shifting Hermit at 80,21, cave Hermit) stay deliberately remote — isolation is
their character.

## Lore/color NPCs (the grey ones)

Optional, no quests, pure flavor and rumor. The convention:

- **Color `#8f938f`** (dust grey) — visually reads as "optional".
- **Symbol `~`** — quiet chatter, distinct from quest `!`/`★` markers.
- Dialog is 1–2 nodes: one line of place-lore plus one rumor that points at
  real content with a reward attached (Echo Relay riches, the sealed dome,
  the toll road), so even color speech pulls the player outward.

Current roster: Keeper Odessa (Dry Bell), Cook Sable & Old Marek (Sparrow's
Rest), Pilgrim Ashe & Courier Wren (Last Ridge), Cistern-Keeper Dov (Hall
yard), Stoker Imm (Hall interior), Squatter Finn (Ridge bunker steps).

When adding people to the world: put them somewhere they'd survive (water,
walls, a lit door), give travel a reason (rumor forward, memory backward), and
grey them out unless they hand the player a verb.
